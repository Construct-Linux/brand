# Rounds: the site

- **0** Baseline: preview.html at f1a16cc, 11 sections at 1440 and 390, dark and light; two
  batches scored twice by Codex, Claude Sonnet and Claude Opus. Median 6; usability 4 (both
  Claude reviewers), responsive 4-6, identity 8-9. Noise floor 1.5. Broken by consensus: the
  perception table's cut Verdict column, the mobile drawings (a cut prompt, an empty calendar),
  the comparison's orphan column heads on a phone.
- **1** The page as a GNOME window (sidebar on the shelf with labelled controls and the section
  index), an h1 and lead, real h2s, column labels, the perception table fitted and carded on a
  phone, wallpapers at twice the size and linked, paths in mono; the drawings in a sideways
  scroller on a phone. Median 7 (+1.0, inside the 2.5 noise), 73% of pairs. Won 3/3: top (both
  widths), menus desktop, perception (both), wallpapers, identity, accent mobile. Lost 2-3/3: the
  three mobile drawings, cropped by the scroller; Codex alone preferred the wider column of the
  baseline on most desktop sections.
- **2** On a phone each drawing a square with its windows recomposed (no scroller); the first
  drawing's caption under it; swatches by role, six levels of elevation in one row; the line
  samples given a divider; the perception pair's label beside its chip. Median 7 (+1.0, inside
  the 1.5 noise), 91% of pairs, no screen lost by a majority. The three mobile drawings now win
  3/3; Codex alone still prefers the baseline's wider column on two desktop drawings and the
  eyebrow labels on two mobile sections. Still raised: the first drawing below the fold at
  1440x900, the perception tier alone in its column on a phone, tall identity cards on a phone,
  wallpaper file names that are not links while identity's are.
- **3** The h1 heads the first drawing, which now ends above the fold at 1440x900; wallpaper
  files linked like the identity paths; light's grounds apart from status in the swatches; on a
  phone the pair's tier a badge and the identity previews half as tall. Median 7 (+1.0, inside
  the 1.8 noise), 89% of pairs. Lost 2/3: light.desktop.dark - the light Settings window, given a
  max-height to clear the dock, cut its own Cancel and Apply. Codex alone kept its preference for
  the baseline's wider column and four-up wallpapers. Budget of four councils spent.
- **4** (Opus alone) The Settings window as tall as its content, the tagline under the wordmark,
  the mark in the wordmark's colour (light's ink teal, no cyan in light), only the failing figure
  in the error colour, the line samples as a two-row list. 88% of pairs: light.desktop.dark won
  back; light.desktop.light went to the baseline with a reason that praises the candidate (a/b
  confused). The climb stops here: rounds 2-4 moved neither the median nor the win rate beyond
  the noise.
